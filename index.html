Hey, I'm Chase.

Swipe for the real me →

I live on the Coffs Coast. Through the week I'm out with young people chasing their goals, and on weekends you'll probably find me behind the decks at a local pub.

I started Chase Supports to back young people the way I'd want to be backed. Someone who gets your music, your jokes and your plans, pushes you to try new things, and helps you get the skills, the job and the social life you want.
